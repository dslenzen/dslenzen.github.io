I am a graduating senior at La Jolla Country Day School, where I have pursued an interdisciplinary curriculum within the Design and Innovation Program. Over four years I have developed substantive competencies in computational design, physical fabrication, and embedded systems — skills I apply both to original research and to entrepreneurial ventures.

My laboratory experience at The Scripps Research Institute deepened my interest in the empirical sciences, exposing me to established wet-laboratory protocols and the iterative rigor that defines experimental inquiry. Alongside this, I have cultivated a strong foundation in human-centered design and product development, culminating in a market-ready consumer product currently being scaled in partnership with an industry collaborator.

Entering the University of Chicago in Fall 2026, I intend to pursue studies at the intersection of Economics, Psychology, and Innovation — with an openness to contributing to research spanning behavioral science, design methodology, molecular biology, and beyond. I am an adaptable, self-directed collaborator who thrives in intellectually demanding environments.
